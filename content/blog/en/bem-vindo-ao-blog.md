# Welcome to the Cadente blog

This is where we will record what we are building, the decisions that guide the product and the practices that make Cadente more predictable in everyday use.

## What you will find here

- releases and changes that deserve context;
- short guides to get more out of the AI features;
- behind the scenes of architecture, privacy and performance;
- lessons from the community and plans for the next cycles.

The blog follows the same public feed the Launcher reads. When a new post lands here, it shows up in the app's news area after the next feed update.

## Built to last

Cadente brings conversations, code, terminal, Git and focus tools together in one native experience. Our job is to reduce friction without hiding what is happening: every update should be verifiable, reversible and clear to the people who use it.

If you found a problem or have an idea, [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/cadente-hub/cadente-hub.github.io/issues). The next posts will turn those conversations into concrete decisions and improvements.
